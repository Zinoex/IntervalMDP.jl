# 1b.3 block-size sweep: `DENSE_GAP_BLOCK_SIZE` (`src/bellman.jl`)

Constant swept: `DENSE_GAP_BLOCK_SIZE`, the block size `B` of `blocked_gap_value`, the dense O-max budget walk (H6,
REPORT § 12.3). The walk adds a block of `B` consecutive `perm` indices at once when the block's gaps sum to less than
the remaining budget. The block where the budget runs out, and the tail, are finished by the sequential `gap_value`.

**Chosen value: `B = 4`.**

## Method

- **Session.** A single session on 2026-11-03, 18:27–18:53 UTC. Driver: a working-tree swap of the constant. Each
  `(threads, B, round)` ran in its own process:
  `julia --project=benchmark --threads=T benchmark/run.jl --suite full --filter <F> --entries bellman --budget-scale 0.3`.
- **Cases.**
  - 1 thread: `imdp-dense-n{100,1000,4000}-a{1,4}`.
  - 16 threads: `imdp-dense-n{1000,4000}-a4`.
  - Entry: steady-state `bellman` (`upper_bound = false`, `maximize = true`).
- **Block sizes and rounds.**
  - 1 thread: B ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32}, 3 interleaved rounds, with the B order rotated per round (4 16 8 32 / 32 8 16 4 /
    8 4 32 16).
  - 16 threads: one round in the order 4 8 16 32.
  - B ∈ {2, 64, 128} were left out of the final sweep. In an earlier, unsettled 2026-10-28 attempt (archived locally,
    not used here), their geometric-mean ratio to the best B was 1.58–1.67, against 1.07–1.23 for B ∈ {4, 8, 16, 32}.
- **Clock rule.** Every kept process has a settled run-start clock reference: `lib/clock.jl`, probe range ≤ 2%. A
  process with a `NOT settled` reference was discarded and re-run. 34 processes were started, 18 were discarded as
  `NOT settled` and 16 were kept.
- **Raw data (local, gitignored).**
  - Results: `benchmark/results/1b.3-sweep-t<T>-B<B>-r<r>.json`.
  - Logs, environment log and discarded attempts: `benchmark/results/logs/1b.3-sweep/`.
- **Values.** Each value is the geometric mean over the rounds of the per-process `bellman` median, in µs. The number
  in parentheses is the ratio to the best B of the row.

## Environment

- **Host.** Intel Core Ultra 7 255H (6 P, 8 E, 2 LP-E cores, 24 MiB L3), Linux 7.2.10, Julia 1.13.1.
- **Power settings.** `platform_profile` = performance, EPP and governor `performance`, `intel_pstate/no_turbo` = 0,
  AC online, `/sys/fs/cgroup/cpuset.cpus.isolated` empty.
- **Process checks.** No other Julia process at any of the 34 pre-process checks, nor at any post-process check.
- **Pinning.** Compact pinning with ThreadPinning.jl. With 1 thread, the thread runs on CPU 0, a P core. With 16
  threads, they run on CPUs 0–15.
- **Clock.** The run-start clock reference (median of the reference probes) was 196.5 µs in every kept process. The
  probe range was 0.0–1.2%. No record has `clock_state` = deviating (pre-trial `clock_probe_ratio`).
- **Correctness.** The benchmark correctness check passed for every entry of every kept process.

## Choice and rationale

The rule, fixed before this sweep: choose the B that minimises the geometric mean, over the 1-thread cases, of the
per-case ratio to the best B, with ties within 0.5% going to the smaller B. This gives **B = 4** (1.021; next is
B = 8 at 1.085).

- **Small n.** At n = 100 the walk ends after roughly half of the 100 indices (by construction of the generator: budget ≈ 0.3 against a total gap ≈ 0.6). Larger blocks then waste more of the
  block where the budget runs out, and B = 8–32 are 1.22–2.15× slower than B = 4.
- **n ≥ 1000.** B = 4 is within 1.6–4.3% of the best block size (B = 16).
- **16 threads.** B = 4 is within 2.7–4.6% of the best (B = 8), while B = 16 and B = 32 are 1.25–1.46× slower than
  B = 8.

Taken together (1-thread and 16-thread cases), B = 4 also has the lowest geometric mean of the ratios:
1.025 for B = 4, against 1.063 for B = 8.

Choosing per size (for example B = 16 for n ≥ 1000 at 1 thread) would need dispatch on the support size, which is a
separate experiment; this run does not do it.

The constant is defined once, next to a comment that points here: `DENSE_GAP_BLOCK_SIZE` in `src/bellman.jl`.
